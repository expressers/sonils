# Design

Three home-page directions under review. One will be chosen and become the system; until then they share tokens and content.

## Shared foundation

- Brand colours from the SONILS logo: yellow `#ffdc00`, red `#d10a11`, ink `#1d1d1b`. Light pages only (client preference). Red is reserved for safety and emergency.
- Type: Archivo variable (width axis). Width carries the voice per direction; weight steps 560 to 700. Numerals tabular where they are data.
- Motion (Emil Kowalski rules): exponential ease-out entrances, `scale(0.97)` press feedback, hover gated behind `(hover: hover)`, only transform/opacity/clip-path/filter animated, everything collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Lenis smooth scroll + GSAP ScrollTrigger.
- Media: SONILS official photography and satellite frames; Pexels footage for sea and vessel atmosphere. Videos play only while visible.
- Unvalidated figures always carry the "a validar" note.

## A. Relatório Anual (`/a`)
Paper `#f4f4f0` ground, yellow cover field that the hero video plate crosses, report-style figure columns with 2px ink rules, pinned satellite growth sequence 2000 to 2016, sticky service index with a yellow marker highlight, kinetic field-name marquee, yellow colophon footer.

## B. Planta da Base (`/b`)
Technical drawing sheet: white ground, 1.5px ink frames, dimension lines, a title block holding the hero copy over aerial video. Signature: interactive schematic plan of the base (drawn on scroll, zones light yellow, safety zone red) linked to a legend and media inset. Figures as dimension annotations. 2000 vs 2016 comparison slider. Condensed width (72 to 80%).

## C. Fachada (`/c`)
Steel-and-glass partition: 6px ink glazing bars, 14px mullions between bays, clear and seeded panes, yellow and red panes as the only colour notes. Panes start frosted and clear when they enter the viewport; nothing slides. Expanded width (115 to 125%).
