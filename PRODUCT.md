## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite + TypeScript, Bun as package manager/runtime, static deploy on Render (Expressers GitHub org). Chosen by Eládio.

## Users

- Oil & Gas operators (IOCs, Sonangol, national players) and international Oilfield Services companies evaluating or renewing dedicated facilities, quay access and cargo handling at the Luanda base. Procurement, logistics and HSE managers; they read with a checklist mindset.
- Government, institutional partners, delegations and press: they need to grasp scale and national relevance quickly.
- Candidates (careers) and local communities (social responsibility, "SONILS Acreditar").
- Existing residents/clients needing practical access: base entry rules, client portal, contacts, emergency numbers.

## Product Purpose

Institutional website of SONILS (Sonangol Integrated Logistics Services, Lda). It must communicate that SONILS is strategic industrial infrastructure for Angola's offshore Oil & Gas, not a conventional logistics company, and route each audience to what it needs. Success: a visitor leaves with the sense of scale, seriousness and reliability, and a client finds capacity, services and contacts without friction.

## Positioning

The integrated onshore supply base in Luanda: maritime access and dedicated quay, cargo handling, warehousing, engineering, dedicated client facilities, heavy equipment, own utilities (power, industrial water), security and emergency response, specialised Angolan workforce, all inside one self-sufficient perimeter near the commercial port, the international airport and the deep-water fields. More than three decades (inaugurated 1995, feasibility study 1993) supporting landmark offshore developments (Girassol, Dalia, PSVM, Pazflor, Kaombo). Evolving toward an integrated logistics platform with regional relevance.

## Operating Context

24/7 operation. Clients include operators and service companies with dedicated bases inside the perimeter (e.g. TotalEnergies zone, BP Base B, Baker, Halliburton, Schlumberger, Aker Solutions, FMC, Fugro, Expro, GE). Government authorities on site: SIC, Capitania, CUP, Polícia Fiscal, AGT, SME. Annual Safety Day, Safety Run, SOPLIANCE compliance programme.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Figures on the current site are inconsistent (quay 1,733 m vs "2 km"; workforce 877 vs 700+ vs 1,609; "23 years" vs 30 years; 60% of national production). Treat every figure as unvalidated; label provisional figures and flag them for client validation.
- Home page only in this first phase; other routes are placeholders.
- Bilingual PT/EN expected long term; PT first.

## Brand Commitments

- Name SONILS, logo: yellow disc with red/black "S" and circular arrows (red + black). Brand colours yellow, red, black.
- Client preference: light, bright websites, not dark. Serious, institutional, visually very strong, cinematic allowed.

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles

1. Scale before slogans: show the infrastructure, then name it.
2. Safety is the licence to operate; it is never decoration.
3. Every audience reaches its door in one move.
4. Angolan capacity (Local Content) is part of the proof, not a footnote.
5. Unverified numbers are labelled, never invented.
